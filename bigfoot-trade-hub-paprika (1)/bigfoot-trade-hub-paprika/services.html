Bring food into Singapore

Import

We source food items from overseas suppliers and bring them into Singapore, with documentation and receipt planned from the start.

  • Supplier sourcing to your specification
  • Import documents and food permits coordinated
  • Receipt into storage with Bigfoot Group
  • Release to you or onward to buyers

Re-export

Paperwork

Documents
We Look After.

We help prepare and check what a food shipment needs. Depending on the product and route, that can include:

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Certificate of origin
  • Health or phytosanitary certificates, where required
  • Singapore import permits and food licences
  • Bill of lading or air waybill
  • Export declarations for re-export

FAQ

Good
Questions.

Still unsure? Ask us directly.

What products do you trade?

Mainly rice, nuts and spices. We can also look at other food items, so tell us what you need.

Is there a minimum order?

It depends on the product and the trade route. Share your volume and we’ll tell you what’s practical.

Can I get samples?

Ask about samples when you enquire and we’ll let you know what’s possible for that product.

Do you handle shipping and paperwork?

Yes. We work with Bigfoot Group’s logistics companies for storage, freight and customs, so trade and logistics are planned together.

Which markets do you serve?

We’re based in Singapore, supplying local buyers and re-exporting to overseas markets. Tell us where the goods need to go.
