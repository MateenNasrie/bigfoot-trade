Our story

Trade connects people, places and the food on our tables.

Bigfoot Trade Hub is the Bigfoot Group’s food trading business. We import, re-export and supply rice, nuts and spices from Singapore, connecting overseas supply with local markets.

Being part of a logistics group means the storage, freight and customs teams are already on our side, so every order is planned from source to shelf.
